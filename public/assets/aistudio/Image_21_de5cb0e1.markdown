## Brand & Style
The brand voice combines artisanal furniture craftsmanship with modern, data-dense enterprise resource planning. It rejects cold, generic SaaS aesthetics in favor of Warm Minimalism—an editorial, deliberate visual language reminiscent of architectural monographs and high-end atelier catalogs. 

The target audience comprises furniture business founders, operations directors, supply chain managers, and inventory accountants who manage high-value physical goods. The user interface must evoke:
- **Quiet Authority:** Confident, structured, and deliberate without screaming for attention.
- **Material Tactility:** Deep architectural woods, natural woven fibers, and refined raw paper textures translated into high-clarity digital surfaces.
- **Operational Clarity:** High-density financial figures, inventory states, and production workflows organized with rigorous typographic and tabular alignment.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in the natural tones of timber processing, leather upholstery, and architectural linen. It balances low-strain ergonomics for 8-hour desktop operational work with rich, grounding contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#795740` - Walnut Brown):** Primary call-to-actions, focused elements, key metric headers, and active high-level indicators.
- **Secondary (`#4A3428` - Dark Espresso Brown):** Primary navigation sidebar, deep mastheads, persistent framing anchors, and high-emphasis display typographic accents.
- **Tertiary (`#B58A63` - Caramel):** Interactive hover states, subtle accent badges, active tab underlines, and progress milestones.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8F5F0` - Warm Ivory):** Global viewport background, modal underlays, and canvas foundation. Replaces stark blue-cast grays with soft natural light.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Discrete cards, data tables, sheets, and active field fills.
- **Structural Border (`#E8E0D7` - Sand Linen):** Low-contrast architectural hairline dividers and container outlines.

### Semantic Accents (Muted Financial Indicators)
- **Cash Inflow / Positive / Success:** `#5E7A60` (Muted Loden Green) with tint `#EDF2ED` and stroke `#C7D6C8`.
- **Cash Outflow / Danger / Critical:** `#A85348` (Terracotta Brick) with tint `#F9EFEB` and stroke `#E2C0BC`.
- **Pending / Attention / In-Production:** `#B8833B` (Raw Ochre) with tint `#FBF4E8` and stroke `#E8D5B5`.

## Typography
The system employs a dual-font structure: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides architectural warmth and sculpted clarity for editorial headers, section summaries, and card titles. **Inter** handles data-dense body copy, metadata labels, inputs, and all financial metrics.

### Financial Figures & Currency
All currency displays (Indonesian Rupiah / `Rp`), volume units (m³, pcs), ledger values, and inventory SKU counts must enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`) to ensure perfect vertical decimal and digit alignment across table rows. Indonesian Rupiah currency identifiers (`Rp`) are rendered at medium weight with a non-breaking half-space separating the symbol from the quantity (e.g., `Rp 148.250.000`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates on a strict 8px baseline grid with a 4px sub-grid for dense financial data tables and micro-controls. 

### Grid & Structure
- **Persistent Shell:** 260px fixed Dark Espresso (`#4A3428`) sidebar navigation on desktop (collapsible to 72px icon-rail), adjacent to a fluid canvas.
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px outer margins, 16px standard gutters. High-density data tables utilize 8px compact gutters (`gutter-compact`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px margins. Sidebar transitions to an overlay drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px margins. Multi-column financial ledgers convert into card lists with right-aligned value metrics.

### Vertical Rhythms
Sections, financial summaries, and inventory stages are separated by exact multiples of 8px. Metric cards, form fields, and toolbar controls maintain explicit vertical heights: 32px (dense table control), 40px (default operational button/input), and 48px (major touchpoint action).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is established primarily through **tonal layering and crisp hairline outlines** rather than blurred, heavy drop shadows. This reflects the precision of woodworking blueprints and ledger sheets.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F0`), completely flat.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` framed with a 1px solid border in Sand Linen (`#E8E0D7`). Flat, with no shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover / Popover):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#E8E0D7`) and a warm ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(74, 52, 40, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(74, 52, 40, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals / Slide-over Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` with a subtle deep anchor shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(74, 52, 40, 0.12), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(74, 52, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Underlay Backdrop:** `#4A3428` tinted with 40% opacity and a 2px backdrop blur for modal dialogs.

## Shapes
A "Soft" corner language (`roundedness: 1`) is enforced across the interface. Geometry is restrained and architectural, echoing precise mitred joinery and honed stone edges rather than casual mobile consumer curves.

- **Micro elements (Checkboxes, badge tags, table pills):** 2px to 4px border radius.
- **Standard controls (Inputs, buttons, dropdown triggers):** 4px (`rounded`) border radius.
- **Containers (Cards, slide-out panels, tables, data dialogs):** 6px to 8px (`rounded-lg`) border radius.
- **Pill overrides:** Forbidden except for inline status dots or circular item counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Walnut Brown (`#795740`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, 4px border radius. Hover: `#664935`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#B58A63`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure White background, 1px border in `#E8E0D7`, text in `#4A3428`. Hover: `#F8F5F0` background with border `#D4C7BA`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Transparent background, text in `#795740`. Hover: `#F8F5F0` background.
- **Destructive:** Pure White background, 1px border in `#E2C0BC`, text in `#A85348`. Hover: `#F9EFEB`.

### Data Cards & Financial KPI Tiles
Cards use a `#FFFFFF` fill with a continuous 1px `#E8E0D7` hairline border. KPI tiles present the metric label in `label-sm` uppercase tracking (`#795740`), primary value in `financial-xl` tabular Inter, and secondary trend badges using the semantic green/red tints. Cards must never use heavy drop shadows.

### Financial Tables & Data Grids
- **Header:** Background `#F8F5F0`, 36px height, text in `label-sm` (`#795740`), bottom border 1px solid `#E8E0D7`.
- **Rows:** Alternating row striping is omitted. Rows use `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with 1px `#E8E0D7` border separators and hover state in `#FAF8F5`.
- **Numeric Columns:** Indonesian Rupiah amounts and quantity counts are strictly right-aligned with monospace/tabular digits (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`).

### Input Fields & Selects
- 40px default height (32px for in-table editing).
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, border: 1px solid `#E8E0D7`, text: `#4A3428`, placeholder: `#9E8E81`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#795740` with an outer ring: `0 0 0 1px #795740`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- 20px height, 4px corner radius, horizontal padding of 6px, `label-sm` font.
- **Inflow / Completed:** Background `#EDF2ED`, text `#5E7A60`, border 1px solid `#C7D6C8`.
- **Outflow / Overdue:** Background `#F9EFEB`, text `#A85348`, border 1px solid `#E2C0BC`.
- **Pending / In Production:** Background `#FBF4E8`, text `#B8833B`, border 1px solid `#E8D5B5`.
- **Neutral / Draft:** Background `#F8F5F0`, text `#795740`, border 1px solid `#E8E0D7`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 16px × 16px, 2px border radius for checkboxes, circle for radios.
- Border: 1.5px solid `#B58A63`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: `#795740` background with crisp white check mark icon.
